FAQ

What is Karta?

Karta is an all-in-one payment app. You can fund your balance with supported top-up methods, manage your digital assets, use your Visa card for everyday purchases, and handle payments in just a few clicks. Whether you're shopping online or in-store, Karta makes the experience simple and seamless.

Are there any fees for registration or getting the card?

Signing up is free, and you can get started right away. There's a one-time fee of 30 USDT 5 USDT for issuing your virtual Visa Signature® card.

Are there any fees for using the card or account?

For a full breakdown of applicable fees, please refer to our dedicated article

How do I get the card?

Just start the Karta Telegram bot , open the app within Telegram, complete a quick verification (it takes up to 5 minutes). After that, top up your balance to activate your virtual card — that's all!

How can I fund my account?

Stablecoins: USDT or USDC on Tron (TRC-20), Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Solana. Bank transfer: Coming soon.

How do I withdraw my funds?

Just tap the Send button in the app to move your money to an external crypto wallet. Bank withdrawals are coming soon — stay tuned!

How do I contact customer support?

Need help? Just reach out to us via the in-app chat for fast, friendly support! For answers to common questions, visit our Help Center .
